Section 01: Physical Prepaid SIM Specifications & Compatibility
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Product Name       : Changi Recommends Prepaid Overseas Travel SIM Card.
* Primary Function   : Provides low-cost cellular data connectivity on local 
                       telecommunication networks abroad without international 
                       roaming fees.
* Card Form Factor   : 3-in-1 multi-fit punch card (Standard, Micro, and Nano).
* Hardware Support   : Factory-unlocked smartphones, cellular tablets, and mobile 
                       hotspots. Carrier-locked devices cannot be used.
* Supported Services :
  - Mobile Data       : Full high-speed 4G / 5G connectivity for apps and web.
  - Cellular Voice/SMS: Not supported. SIMs are data-only.
  - VoIP / OTT Calling: Fully supported (WhatsApp, FaceTime, LINE, WeChat, 
                        Telegram, Zoom, Skype).
  - Social Media Apps : Fully supported (including TikTok, subject to local 
                        country regulations).
* Personal Hotspot   : Tethering permissions vary by individual destination 
                       package. Verify packaging before tethering multiple devices.
* Plan Continuity    : Once activated abroad, the calendar-day validity counter 
                       runs continuously and cannot be paused or split.
* Shelf Life         : Unactivated physical SIM cards expire 1 year from the 
                       date of purchase.


Section 02: Physical SIM Activation Protocols & APN Configuration
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Critical Rule: DO NOT insert or test the SIM card inside Singapore or during 
  layovers. Insert the SIM ONLY after physical landing in the destination country.
* Dual-SIM Handsets:
  - Always place the Travel SIM into SIM Slot 1.
  - Temporarily turn off or disable your domestic primary SIM to prevent accidental 
    background data roaming charges from your home carrier.

* Step-by-Step Activation for iOS (Apple iPhone / iPad):
  1. Insert SIM into the SIM tray upon arrival at your overseas destination.
  2. Open "Settings" > "Cellular" (or "Mobile Data").
  3. Toggle ON "Cellular Data" and select the Travel SIM line.
  4. Tap "Cellular Data Options" and toggle ON "Data Roaming".
  5. Manual APN Setup (if no connection appears within 3 minutes):
     - Navigate to: "Settings" > "Mobile Data" > "Mobile Data Network".
     - In the "APN" field under Cellular Data, enter: mobiledata
     - Leave Username and Password completely blank.
     - Toggle Airplane Mode ON for 10 seconds, then toggle OFF.

* Step-by-Step Activation for Android (Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi, Oppo, etc.):
  1. Insert SIM into Slot 1 upon arrival.
  2. Open "Settings" > "Connections" (or "Network & Internet") > "SIM Manager".
  3. Verify SIM 1 is enabled and set as the Preferred SIM for Mobile Data.
  4. Tap "Mobile Networks" and toggle ON "Data Roaming".
  5. Manual APN Setup (if no connection appears within 3 minutes):
     - Navigate to: "Settings" > "Connections" > "Mobile Networks" > "Access Point Names".
     - Tap "Add" or the "+" icon to create a new APN.
     - Name: mobiledata
     - APN : mobiledata
     - Leave Proxy, Port, Username, and Password blank (Not Set).
     - Tap the three dots (top right) > "Save".
     - Select the new "mobiledata" APN profile to make it active.
     - Toggle Airplane Mode ON for 10 seconds, then toggle OFF.


Section 03: Travel eSIM Overview, Installation Rules & Single-Scan Mandate
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Product Category   : Digital Embedded SIM (eSIM).
* Delivery Method    : Sent via email immediately upon payment confirmation. 
                       Includes an installation QR code and ICCID number.
* Hardware Pre-Check : The customer's smartphone must be carrier-unlocked and 
                       support eSIM hardware (e.g., iPhone XS or newer, Google 
                       Pixel 3 or newer, Samsung Galaxy S20/Fold/Flip or newer).
* CRITICAL SINGLE-SCAN POLICY:
  - Each eSIM QR code is strictly SINGLE-USE.
  - One eSIM can only be activated on one single mobile device.
  - NEVER remove or delete an eSIM profile from device settings during troubleshooting.
  - A deleted eSIM profile CANNOT be reinstalled, re-scanned, or recovered.
  - If a user deletes the profile, an entirely new eSIM must be purchased.
* Installation Timing:
  - Highly recommended to scan and install the eSIM on the date of departure while 
    connected to a stable Wi-Fi connection.
  - QR Code Validity: QR code must be scanned within 60 calendar days of purchase.
  - Activation Trigger: Travel eSIMs are activated once the QR code is scanned into 
    the device.
  - Data Roaming and Cellular Data switching for the eSIM line should be turned 
    ON ONLY upon arrival in the destination country.

Section 05: Step-by-Step Device Setup & Manual APN Entry (iOS & Android)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* iOS Configuration SOP (Apple Devices):
  1. Primary Setup:
     - Open "Settings" > "Cellular" (or "Mobile Data").
     - Tap "Cellular Data" and select the Travel eSIM line.
     - Turn OFF "Allow Cellular Data Switching".
     - Turn OFF Data Roaming on your primary domestic SIM line.
     - Select the Travel eSIM profile and toggle ON "Data Roaming".
  2. Manual APN Entry (if no internet connection appears):
     - Tap "Settings" > "Mobile Data" > select the Travel eSIM line.
     - Tap "Mobile Data Network" (or "Cellular Data Network").
     - In the APN field under "Cellular Data", type the destination APN value 
       (e.g., vmobile.jp, ctm-mobile, CTM-mobile, mobiledata, plus).
     - Leave Username and Password blank.
     - Restart device or toggle Airplane Mode ON for 10 seconds, then OFF.

* Android Configuration SOP (Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi, etc.):
  1. Primary Setup:
     - Open "Settings" > "Connections" > "SIM Manager".
     - Ensure the Travel eSIM is toggled ON and set as the Preferred SIM for Mobile Data.
     - Open "Mobile Networks" and ensure "Data Roaming" is enabled for the eSIM.
  2. Manual APN Entry (if no internet connection appears):
     - Open "Settings" > "Connections" > "Mobile Networks" > "Access Point Names".
     - Select the Travel eSIM tab.
     - Tap "Add" or "+" to create a new profile.
     - Set "Name" to the specified APN value.
     - Set "APN" to the exact matching string.
     - Leave Username, Password, Proxy, and Port blank (Not Set).
     - Tap the three dots (top right) > "Save".
     - Tap the radio button next to the new APN to select it.
     - Toggle Airplane Mode ON for 10 seconds, then OFF.


Section 06: Data Bundles, FUP Throttling & Live Balance Enquiry Portal
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Plan Validity Calculation: 24-Hour Periods vs. Calendar Day Reset:
  1. Majority of Countries (24-Hour Period Format):
     - Validity is calculated in exact 24-hour periods from the specific time of activation.
     - Example (4-day Japan eSIM): Activated at 1:33 PM on 1 Jan -> Expires at 1:32 PM on 5 Jan.
  2. Thailand & Vietnam Plans (Calendar Day Reset):
     - Validity resets daily at 00:00 Hrs (midnight) local time, regardless of time zone.
     - Expiration is determined by calendar days, NOT a 24-hour clock.
     - Example (1-day Thailand/Vietnam): Activated at 2:00 PM (14:00 Hrs) -> Expires at 
       11:59 PM (23:59 Hrs) on the same day.
     - Example (3-day Thailand): Activated at 2:30 PM on 1 Jan -> Ends at 11:59 PM on 3 Jan.
* Fair Usage Policy (FUP) & Throttling:
  - Daily Quotas: 500MB, 1GB, 2GB, or 3GB high-speed data tiers.
  - Fixed Quotas: Fixed total high-speed volume for the full duration (e.g., 10GB for 15 days).
  - Throttled Speed: When high-speed data is exhausted, speeds throttle to an unlimited 
    128 kbps for basic messaging and maps until the next reset.
* Live Data Balance Check Portal:
  - Enquiry Portal URL: https://www.changirecommends.com/esim_enquiry.aspx
  - Verification Method: Input the 19- or 20-digit SIM / eSIM ICCID number to 
    view live data usage, remaining high-speed data, and expiry dates.
* Dedicated Support Channels:
  - Travel eSIM WhatsApp Support: +65 8790 0737 (Text Only)
  - Customer Service Email     : call@changirecommends.com.sg


Section 07: Definition of "Unlimited Data (High-Speed XXGB Daily)"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Core Definition:
  - A Travel eSIM with an "Unlimited Data (High-Speed XXGB Daily)" package consists 
    of two components:
    1. A fixed daily allotment of full high-speed data (e.g., 500MB, 1GB, 2GB, or 3GB).
    2. Endless lowered-speed data at 128 kbps once the daily high-speed quota is used up 
       (subject to Fair Use Policy).
* How the Daily Reset Works:
  - Each day starts with the full high-speed data allotment.
  - Once the initial daily high-speed quota is exhausted, connection remains active 
    with unlimited data throttled down to 128 kbps.
  - The high-speed data quota resets every day for the total purchased duration of the plan.
* Practical Example (Australia & New Zealand 2GB High-Speed Daily, Unlimited Data for 3 Days):
  - The user receives 2GB of high-speed data per day + endless lowered-speed data (128 kbps) 
    after the initial 2GB is consumed.
  - The 2GB high-speed data allotment resets each day across all 3 days of the plan.


Section 08: Comprehensive Travel eSIM Operational FAQs & Guidelines
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* When should I activate my eSIM?
  - Highly recommended to scan the QR code on the date of departure using a stable Wi-Fi connection.
  - Travel eSIMs activate once scanned. QR codes must be scanned within 60 days of purchase.
  - Upon arrival at destination, turn on Data Roaming and restart device if necessary.
* How long does activation take?
  - Typically takes up to 30 minutes depending on device model and internet speed. Stable Wi-Fi is required.
* What do "Activating eSIM" or "Unable to Activate eSIM" notifications mean?
  - Common when scanned before reaching the destination country. The profile becomes fully 
    active once the device connects to the local destination network upon arrival.
* When should Data Roaming be enabled?
  - Turn Data Roaming ON only upon arrival at the destination country.
  - Remember to turn OFF data roaming on personal domestic SIMs to prevent home carrier charges.
* Should APN settings be preset before traveling?
  - No. It is not advisable to preset APN values before arrival. Update upon landing if required.
* Can I use Personal Hotspot / Tethering with an eSIM?
  - Yes, hotspot/tethering is supported provided the customer's phone model supports it.
* Can I send or receive SMS with an eSIM?
  - No. Changi Travel eSIMs are strictly data-only; cellular SMS and voice calls are unavailable. 
    Use VoIP apps (WhatsApp, FaceTime, Telegram, WeChat).
* Can the same QR code be used on multiple devices?
  - No. One eSIM can only be activated on a single device once. It cannot be transferred or shared.
* How do I remove a used eSIM?
  - iOS    : Settings > Cellular (Mobile Data) > select Travel eSIM > Remove Cellular Plan.
  - Android: Settings > Connections > SIM Card Manager > select eSIM > Remove Mobile Plan.
* Can I remove and reactivate an eSIM?
  - No. eSIMs can only be added and activated once. Once removed/deleted, it is permanently void.
  - To save battery/data, toggle the eSIM line OFF (disable temporarily) instead of deleting it.
* When is it best to remove the eSIM profile?
  - Remove only after the data package has fully expired, travel is completed, or when you are 
    no longer in the destination country.
* Why is the connection speed not running on 5G?
  - 5G access depends on local network coverage and device hardware compatibility. Devices 
    automatically switch to 4G/LTE in areas without 5G coverage.
* Why does device data tracking differ from the Changi balance portal?
  - Handset data counters (especially iOS) often experience a 24-hour delay in reflecting usage. 
    Accurate real-time usage should always be verified at: https://www.changirecommends.com/esim_enquiry.aspx
* Does the eSIM provide 100% full geographic coverage?
  - Wide countrywide coverage is provided, but signal strength may be weaker in remote rural 
    or coastal areas (e.g., Osaka and Hokkaido coastal regions).

Section 44: Comprehensive Hotel Operational FAQs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Geography, Boundaries & Immigration:
   - What are Airside and Landside?
     Airside is the secure passenger transit area after departure immigration and 
     security. Only ticketed passengers with valid boarding passes can enter. 
     Checked luggage cannot be brought airside. Landside is the public area 
     before passport control.
   - Where are the transit hotels located?
     Inside the airside transit areas of Terminals 1, 2, and 3.
   - Do transit passengers clear Singapore immigration to access the hotel?
     No. Transiting passengers stay airside without clearing immigration.
   - Can travelers arriving from Singapore city stay at the hotel?
     Yes, provided their airline provides Early Check-In (ECI), allowing them to 
     collect a boarding pass and pass immigration into the transit area.
   - Is a Singapore entry visa required?
     Passengers remaining strictly in the airside transit area do not need a visa.
   - Can guests stay here if their flight departs from Terminal 4?
     Yes. Guests can stay in T1, T2, or T3 and transfer to Terminal 4 via the 
     complimentary 24/7 airside shuttle bus.

B. Bookings, Payments & Identification:
   - Are walk-in guests accepted?
     Yes, walk-ins are accepted subject to real-time room availability.
   - How are reservations confirmed?
     Confirmation emails are issued by Changi Recommends or your booking agent.
   - How do I amend or cancel an OTA booking?
     Contact your third-party booking agent directly under their terms.
   - What payment modes are accepted at the front desk?
     Major credit cards, debit cards, cash (SGD), and selected foreign currencies.
   - Why is a credit card guarantee required?
     A credit card pre-authorization guarantee secures the room inventory.
   - Why must passports and boarding passes be presented?
     Verification is required under Singapore aviation security regulations.
   - Why are flight details required at booking?
     Flight numbers and arrival times allow front office staff to track delays 
     and prepare rooms accordingly.

C. Stay Durations, Extensions & Flight Delays:
   - How are check-in and check-out times scheduled?
     Stays are booked in flexible hourly blocks based on your confirmation.
   - What is the minimum stay duration?
     Standard minimum block is 6 hours (or 3-hour day-use slots from 09:00 to 17:00).
   - Can I extend my stay while at the hotel?
     Yes, requests can be made at the front desk subject to room availability 
     and applicable hourly extension fees.
   - Are early check-ins or late check-outs permitted?
     Subject to room availability and standard hourly extension charges.
   - What happens if my flight is delayed or cancelled?
     Bookings cover the confirmed hourly blocks. Contact the hotel front desk 
     or support to adjust times based on airline schedule changes.

D. Luggage, Accessibility, Families & Pets:
   - Can I access checked-in luggage during my stay?
     No. Checked bags remain in the airport baggage handling system. Guests can 
     only access hand-carry cabin baggage.
   - Does the hotel store cabin bags before check-in or after check-out?
     No. For airport security reasons, luggage holding and storage services are 
     not provided.
   - Are wheelchair-accessible rooms available?
     Yes, accessible rooms are available. Request at least 48 hours in advance.
   - What is the policy regarding children?
     Children under 18 must be accompanied by an adult, subject to room occupancy limits.
   - Are family rooms available?
     Yes, but inventory is limited. Advance booking is strongly recommended.
   - Are pets permitted?
     No. Animals and pets are strictly prohibited from hotel premises.
   - Where do I report lost personal belongings?
     Items left behind are logged and handed over to the central Changi Airport 
     Information Counter.

Section 55: Product Overview, Pass Durations & Car Classes (Ordinary vs Green)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Product Name       : Japan Rail Pass (JR Pass) - Nationwide & Regional.
* Rail Network Scope : Provides unlimited travel across the 6 Japan Railways Group 
                       (JR Group) networks (JR Hokkaido, JR East, JR Central, 
                       JR West, JR Shikoku, and JR Kyushu).
* Itinerary Guidance :
  - Single-Region Trips: A regional pass (e.g., JR Hokkaido, JR Kansai) is 
    recommended if travel is confined to one area.
  - Multi-Region Trips : The Nationwide Pass is recommended for long-distance travel 
    across multiple Japanese regions.
* Pass Formats:
  1. Nationwide Pass Durations: 7, 14, or 21 consecutive calendar days.
  2. Flexible Regional Passes : Certain regional passes offer flexible use days 
     (e.g., 4 selected days of travel within a 6-day window).
* Carriage Class Tiers:
  - Ordinary Car (Standard Class): Economy seating (2-3 layout on Shinkansen).
  - Green Car (First Class - "Green Pass"): Premium carriage marked with a green 
    four-leaf clover icon. Features larger reclining seats, generous legroom, 
    audio controls, and dedicated luggage space. Highly recommended during peak 
    holiday seasons (May Golden Week, August Obon, September Silver Week).


Section 56: Eligibility Criteria, Dual Nationality & Third-Party Purchases
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Eligibility Mandate:
  - Open strictly to foreign passport holders visiting Japan under the official 
    immigration status of "Temporary Visitor" (Short-Term Tourist Stay).
  - Passports MUST receive the physical "Temporary Visitor" entry stamp or sticker 
    upon arrival. Entering via automated biometric gates without obtaining this 
    stamp invalidates the pass, and no refunds will be granted.
* Ineligible Travelers:
  - Japanese citizens (unless holding documented 10+ year permanent residency abroad).
  - Non-Japanese visitors entering on work, student, military, diplomatic, or cultural visas.
* Dual Nationality Rules: Dual citizens (including Japanese dual nationals) MUST 
  enter Japan using their foreign, non-Japanese passport with a Temporary Visitor stamp.
* Third-Party Purchases: Customers may purchase passes on behalf of others provided 
  the traveler's name matches their passport exactly and they meet JR tourist criteria.
* Non-Transferability: Passes are strictly personal and non-transferable. The 
  traveler must be physically present at the JR Office in Japan to exchange the voucher.


Section 57: Physical MCO vs. Electronic EO Booking & Fulfillment Workflows
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Product Format Differentiation:
  - Customers must check whether their pass is tagged as an "Electronic EO" or is 
    a standard Physical Exchange Order (Physical MCO).

1. Electronic Exchange Order (Electronic EO):
   - Product Tag: Specifically labeled as "(Electronic EO)" in the product listing.
   - Issuance SLA: Available for digital download directly from the customer's 
     Changi Recommends account 2 days after order completion and payment.
   - Airport Collection: CANNOT be picked up as a physical voucher at airport counters. 
     The customer downloads and prints/saves the digital voucher.

2. Physical Exchange Order (Physical MCO):
   - Applicable Passes: Nationwide JR Passes and regional passes not tagged as Electronic EO.
   - Format: Secure paper voucher printed and issued in Singapore before departure.
   - Digital Copy Warning: Electronic copies, invoices, or screenshots are NOT accepted 
     by JR offices in Japan. The original physical paper voucher is mandatory.
   - Airport Collection: Pick up 24/7 at Changi Recommends Departure Hall counters 
     across Terminals 1, 2, 3, and 4 (Public Area).


Section 58: Collection Rescheduling, 6-Month Extensions & Amendment Fees
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Booking Name Accuracy: Traveler names must match their passport details exactly. 
  Changi Recommends is not liable for incorrect details entered during booking.

  - Corrections requested before departure are reviewed at Changi Recommends' discretion.

  - Processing Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM SGT (excluding Singapore

Section 59: Japan Rail Office Exchange SOP, 30-Day Window & Calendar Days
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Step 1: Arrive in Japan: Obtain the physical "Temporary Visitor" stamp at immigration.
* Step 2: Visit a JR Ticket Office / Travel Service Center:
  - Locations: Major airports (Narita, Haneda, Kansai, New Chitose, Fukuoka) and 
    central train stations (Tokyo, Shinjuku, Kyoto, Shin-Osaka, Nagoya, Hiroshima).
  - Submit: Original Physical MCO (or Electronic EO) and Original Passport.
  - Fill out the brief application form.
* Step 3: Select Activation Date:
  - The traveler designates their preferred starting date when exchanging the voucher.
  - The starting date must fall within 30 days of the exchange date.
* Step 4: Validity Calculation (Full Calendar Days):
  - Pass validity is calculated in full calendar days, NOT 24-hour periods.
  - Example: Boarding a train at 9:00 AM on Day 1 and 8:00 AM on Day 2 counts as 
    two separate full calendar days of usage.
  - Consecutive Days: The validity period runs continuously from the starting date.


Section 60: Rail Scope, Bullet Train Exclusions, Ferry & Local Bus Lines
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Train Networks Fully Included:
  - All JR Group rail lines nationwide: JR Hokkaido, JR East, JR Central, 
    JR West, JR Shikoku, and JR Kyushu.
  - Shinkansen lines: Hikari, Sakura, Kodama, Tsubame, Hayabusa, Komachi, Kagayaki, 
    Hakutaka, Asama, Toki, and Tanigawa lines.
  - Limited Express, Express, Rapid, and Local commuter trains.
  - Tokyo Monorail line (Haneda Airport to Hamamatsucho Station).
* Excluded Services:
  - Nozomi (Tokaido/Sanyo) and Mizuho (Sanyo/Kyushu) Shinkansen trains (require

  - Private non-JR railway lines (Odakyu, Kintetsu, Tobu, Hankyu, etc.).
  - City subways, municipal metros, and urban tram lines.
  - Sleeper cars and private luxury compartments on overnight trains.
  - Non-JR Tracks: If a JR train travels over shared private tracks, the non-JR 
    track fare must be paid separately.
* Eligible JR Local Bus Routes:
  - Local routes operated by: JR Hokkaido Bus, JR Bus Tohoku, JR Bus Kanto,

Section 61: Station Entry, Free Seat Reservations & Wheelchair Bookings
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Station Gantry Entry Procedure:
  - The JR Pass does NOT work in automated ticket turnstiles.
  - Travelers must walk through the staffed glass booth next to the gates, showing 
    their physical pass to the station attendant for visual inspection.
* Complimentary Seat Reservations:
  - Pass holders with reserved seat privileges can reserve seats at SGD $0 / JPY 0 extra charge.
  - How to Book: Present your pass at any Midori-no-Madoguchi ticket office or use 
    designated reserved seat ticketing machines at JR stations.
  - Tip: Book well in advance when traveling in groups to secure adjacent seats.
* Commuter Peak Hours & Holiday Warnings:
  - Daily Peak Commute: 07:00 – 09:00 Hrs and 18:00 – 20:00 Hrs.
  - Public Holidays: Trains book out days in advance during Golden Week (late April/early May), 
    Obon (mid-August), and New Year periods.
* Wheelchair Accessibility SOP:
  - Accessible seats are available on most Shinkansen and Limited Express trains.
  - Advance Booking: Reserve at a Midori-no-Madoguchi counter starting at 10:00 AM, 
    up to 1 month before travel.
  - Boarding Restriction: If accessible seats are fully booked, travel on that train 
    may not be possible.
  - Equipment Rules: Wheelchairs must meet standard dimensions; electric wheelchairs 
    with handle steering wheels are generally not permitted unless meeting strict JR criteria.


Section 62: Onboard Etiquette, Luggage Rules, Pets & 180-Day Refund Policy
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Onboard Dining & Smoking:
  - Dining: Permitted on long-distance JR trains. Regional ekiben bento boxes 
    (from ~1,000 Yen) can be eaten onboard.
  - Smoking: Trains operate strict smoking and non-smoking cars. Smoking is allowed 
    ONLY inside designated smoking cabins on equipped Shinkansen train sets.
* Luggage Dimensions & Baggage Allowances:
  - Standard Luggage: Total dimensions (Length + Width + Height) up to 160 cm 
    do not require a reservation.
  - Oversized Luggage (161 cm to 250 cm): Requires booking a designated "Seat with 
    an Oversized Baggage Area" on Tokaido, Sanyo, and Kyushu Shinkansen lines.
* Pet Transportation Policy:
  - Small animals (small dogs, cats, pigeons) are permitted onboard for an extra fee.
  - Carrier Limits: Must be in a carrier with total dimensions <= 120 cm 
    and total weight (including pet) <= 10 kg, causing no disturbance to passengers.
* Child Fare Regulations:
  - Age 0 to 5 (Infants): Travel free without a ticket on an adult's lap. If an 
    individual seat is required, purchase a Child Pass.
  - Age 6 to 11: Eligible for Child Pass (50% of adult fare).
  - Age 12 and above: Must purchase an Adult Pass.
* Lost or Stolen Pass Policy: Passes CANNOT be replaced or reissued if lost or 
  stolen. Travelers must buy a new pass or regular tickets.
* Cancellation & Refund Terms:
  - Singapore Returns: Unused physical Exchange Orders can be returned within 
    180 days of purchase if not turned in in Japan. A 10% administrative fee 
    applies, with a 90-day processing window.
  - Japan Returns: Physical passes can be refunded at a JR Pass office in Japan 
    ONLY before the designated start date, subject to JR administrative deductions. 
    No refunds are granted once the start date has passed.
